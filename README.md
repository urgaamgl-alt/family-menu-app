# Family Menu App

A weekly meal planner that generates family menus based on individual food preferences and ratings.

## Features

- 👥 **Family Member Profiles** - Add family members and track their food preferences
- ⭐ **Food Ratings** - Rate ingredients (fruits, vegetables, meats) and specific foods
- 📅 **Weekly Menus** - View AI-generated menus tailored to family preferences
- 🛒 **Shopping Lists** - Auto-generated shopping list from weekly menu
- 📱 **Mobile Ready** - Works perfectly on iPhone and Android

## Quick Start: Deploy to Vercel in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Download These Files
All the files you need are already in this folder:
- `package.json`
- `pages/index.js`
- `.gitignore`
- `next.config.js`
- `README.md`

### Step 2: Create a GitHub Account (Free)
1. Go to github.com and sign up (free)
2. Confirm your email

### Step 3: Upload Files to GitHub
1. After signing in to GitHub, click the "+" icon in the top right
2. Click "New repository"
3. Name it "family-menu-app"
4. Click "Create repository"
5. GitHub will show you instructions. Ignore those.
6. Instead, look for a button that says "uploading an existing project"
7. Follow their simple upload steps to add these files

### Step 4: Connect GitHub to Vercel
1. Go to vercel.com
2. Click "Sign Up" and choose "Continue with GitHub"
3. Allow Vercel to access your GitHub
4. On the Vercel dashboard, click "New Project"
5. Find and select your "family-menu-app" repository
6. Click "Import"
7. Vercel will auto-detect it's a Next.js project ✓
8. Click "Deploy"
9. **Wait 2-3 minutes** for deployment to finish

### Step 5: Get Your Live Link
Once deployment is done:
1. You'll see a "Visit" button - click it
2. Your app is now LIVE on the internet!
3. You'll get a link like: `https://family-menu-app-xyz123.vercel.app`

### Step 6: Share with Family
1. Share the link with family members
2. They open it on their phone
3. They tap the share button → "Add to Home Screen"
4. App appears on their home screen like a real app

## Important Notes

**It's completely free!** Vercel offers free hosting for Next.js projects.

**No credit card needed** for basic usage.

**The app works offline** once they've visited it once and "installed" it.
